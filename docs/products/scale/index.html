SCALE

A machine-readable framework for telling the platform story: how engineering best practices are Standardized, Consumed, Automated, Leveraged, and made Effective across an organization.

The Five Aspects

Every metric is tagged with exactly one aspect. SCALE is an aggregation and narrative layer over detailed maturity metrics — not another metrics catalog. Maturity frameworks make it cheap to generate hundreds of metrics, and cheap metrics destroy the story. SCALE's purpose is to make hundreds of metrics readable as five aspect-level storylines.

Letter Aspect Question it answers
S Standards Do executable engineering standards exist?
C Consumption Are teams using them (adoption) and using them correctly (conformance)?
A Automation How much is enforced automatically?
L Leverage What reuse and engineering capacity do they create?
E Effectiveness Did engineering and business outcomes improve?

Why It Matters for ProductBuilders

AI agents make it cheap to generate code and specs — they do not make it cheap to keep the result standardized, automated, or reused. Left unchecked, that gap is exactly how AI-generated systems become unmaintainable at scale: consumption outruns standards, automation lags enforcement, and duplicated effort erodes leverage. SCALE exists to keep that gap visible, aspect by aspect, so "did this actually get more maintainable" has an honest answer instead of a metrics dashboard nobody reads.

SCALE is consumed by VisionStudio's Maturity Model panel and is designed to sit alongside deeper assessment frameworks like PRISM — PRISM assesses, SCALE narrates.
