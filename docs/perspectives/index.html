Perspective John Wang · 2026-08-06

Everyone at the Frontier Built Their Own Governor

For most of this year, the tools to govern AI coding agents didn't exist yet — so the people pushing hardest built their own. Now the market is racing to catch up.

Spotify built Honk. Steve Yegge built Gas Town. I built VisionStudio. The pattern is not vanity — when the tools to govern AI agents at scale did not exist yet, the frontier rolled its own, and the tool you ship is the one you build after the problem finally teaches you its shape. Now the market is catching up.
